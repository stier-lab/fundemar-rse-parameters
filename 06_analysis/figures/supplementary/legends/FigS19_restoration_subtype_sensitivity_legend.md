# Fig. S5 (repository asset FigS19): population-source descriptions

(a) Observed interval survival and (b) relative live-area growth rate (yr⁻¹). Open circles are study–region summaries, with point area scaled to record counts; filled diamonds, offset above each row for visibility, are record-weighted subtype means. Survival percentages average binary colony-interval fates. The legacy per-record power transformation leaves 0/1 fates unchanged and does not annualize a cohort survival proportion. Survey durations differ among records, so these percentages are not comparable annual survival rates. Empty growth rows are labeled when no measured live-area data are available. Both panels are descriptive and show no inferential intervals. Legacy resampling intervals are retained in the survival-summary CSV only and do not estimate between-program uncertainty.
Naturally occurring fragments are only NOAA observations explicitly coded fragment = Y, not intact NOAA colonies. In the source integration this designation uses interval-end Colony.Type AF/SF; all such observations therefore have a live endpoint. Their survival estimate is withheld as not estimable from these endpoint-selected records; the 94 observations remain in the inventory and growth summaries. FUNDEMAR fragments were nursery-held on PVC tables, not reef outplants. Pausch records are nursery outplants; Kuffner records are nursery outplants after fragmentation and re-cementation. The origin of USGS outplanted colonies is unspecified. Mendoza-Quiroz sexual cohorts are split between Puerto Morelos Nursery and Cuevones/Picudas reef outplants; they are not asexual fragments.
The subtype inventory contains 1,257 survival records from 6 studies; descriptive survival estimates use 1,163 records after withholding the endpoint-selected natural fragments. Live-area growth includes 857 records from 4 studies. Growth requires positive initial live area and observed live endpoints, available annual live-area change, and positive interval duration; this script makes no total-area substitution. Kuffner and Mendoza-Quiroz growth records lack the required live-area change field and do not enter panel b. The source integration treats surviving USGS colonies as having 100% live cover based on the photographs.
Subtype, study, site, handling, and sample size are not independently balanced; these summaries do not estimate treatment effects or rank restoration methods.
