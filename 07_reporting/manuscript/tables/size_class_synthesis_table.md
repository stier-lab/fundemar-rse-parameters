# Table S3. Size-class synthesis for *Acropora palmata*

This landscape supplementary table links size-structured estimates to their
biological and restoration interpretation. It is designed as a reading table,
not a substitute for the underlying estimates and uncertainty intervals.

Updated for the corrected live-area matrix (2026-10-21; 4,698 growth records,
positive observed live-area endpoints, one-year projection of annualized
live-area change, 0.1-cm² projected endpoint floor). Elasticities below are local
sensitivities of the recruitment-free subsystem, not intervention effects.
Shrinkage and retrogression frequencies are reported separately in their
output-backed subset tables because tissue-loss and matrix eligibility differ.
Size classes do not establish reproductive maturity or skeletal habitat recovery.

| Size class (live area) | Principal risk | Observed demographic pattern | Transition leverage | Restoration implication |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| SC1 (0–10 cm²) | Early mortality; disturbance exposure; sparse coverage outside Florida | Smallest modeled live-area class; not a measured recruitment or maturity state | Aggregated survival elasticity: 0.001 | Track initial survival and subsequent live area; recruitment success requires separate observations |
| SC2 (10–100 cm²) | Post-transplant stress, fragmentation, disease, and backward transitions | Surviving colonies can lose tissue and move to smaller classes | Aggregated survival elasticity: 0.027 | Monitor attachment, survival, and live-area transitions after outplanting |
| SC3 (100–900 cm²) | Partial mortality and disturbance-linked retrogression | Intermediate class with both forward and backward transitions | Aggregated survival elasticity: 0.131 | Follow recovery of live area, not survival alone |
| SC4 (900–4,000 cm²) | Tissue loss, breakage, and loss before reaching the largest class | Connects intermediate and largest live-area classes | Aggregated survival elasticity: 0.254 | Measure retention of live area and movement into SC5; reproductive status requires direct observations |
| SC5 (>4,000 cm²) | Loss or shrinkage of large living colonies; fragmentation | Largest class has the dominant matrix-cell elasticity | Aggregated survival elasticity: 0.534; SC5→SC5 stasis matrix-cell elasticity: 0.514; fragmentation elasticity: 0.046 | Retain large living colonies while monitoring reproduction and skeletal structure separately; elasticity alone cannot rank intervention effectiveness |
